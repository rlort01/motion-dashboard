Updating the entered metrics

  1. Keep entering monthly actuals in "Motion PMG Scorecard - 2026.xlsx", same layout as today.
  2. Save it to OneDrive at Company Documents > Operations > Scorecard (next to the 2027 template). The Monday refresh reads the most recently saved copy with that name.
  3. Type values as plain numbers or percentages; "-" and blanks count as not entered. The refresh reads what the cells display, so show percentages with two decimals.
  4. Need it sooner than Monday? Ask Claude to refresh the dashboard.

How these numbers are worked out
  • Scorecard figures come from AppFolio where it has the metric (marked AppFolio), and otherwise from the 2026 scorecard workbook. Goals are the 2026 goals on that workbook.
  • Combined markets: counts and dollar amounts (doors, owner debt, credits, doors closed, reviews) are summed across Georgia, Tennessee and North Carolina. Rates and times are averaged, weighted by each market's end-of-month doors where all three are entered.
  • 2026 YTD is the average of the months entered so far for rates and times, and the total for counts and dollar amounts. Choose Full year in the month list to see the whole year: the year total or average, how many months met the goal, and the latest month. In full-year view, counts and dollar goals are compared using the monthly average.
  • Insurance compliance entered as whole numbers (for example 96.83 in Georgia for September to November) is read as a percentage.
  • AppFolio data is refreshed every Monday morning. Jobber jobs and invoices refresh hourly on weekdays.
